To my favourite person

Happy Birthday, my love. Sometimes I wish distance was just a silly little thing we could fold up and put away, so I could be beside you today. I'd fix your hair, steal your snacks, make you laugh, and hug you until the whole world went quiet. Until that day comes, please remember: you are loved in all the little moments too — when we're busy, when we're tired, and when all we can do is miss each other through a screen. I hope this year gives you courage for your dreams, peace on difficult days, and so many reasons to smile. If I could wrap one thing for you, it would be the feeling of being loved completely. Happy birthday, my boy. No matter how many miles are between us, I'm always sending a little love your way. All my love, always. ❤️

yours, always ♡
